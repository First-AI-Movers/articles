Bureaucratic and data-processing organizations see AI now performing contract generation, government form completion, and legal support functions that previously required extensive human labor and specialized legal knowledge. These disruptions are not limited to a single sector but are occurring across virtually every industry where routine cognitive tasks can be systematized and automated.

He identifies four distinct chronotypes: Lions, who comprise 10-15% of the population and are most productive in early morning hours; Bears, representing 50-55% of people and functioning optimally with standard 9-to-5 schedules; Wolves, the night owls who perform best in evening hours; and Dolphins, light sleepers with shorter sleep drives who often experience anxiety and obsessive-compulsive tendencies.

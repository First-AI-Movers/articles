The article acknowledges AI limitations including context window constraints requiring human oversight, hallucinations necessitating verification systems, and tool proliferation creating decision paralysis. The core strategic implication is that four-year roadmaps may become obsolete in four months—not because the vision was wrong, but because execution timelines assumed competitive barriers that no longer exist.

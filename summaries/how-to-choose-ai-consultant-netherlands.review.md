The article identifies four distinct decision framings: creating a clear AI priority list and use case sequencing, assessing readiness before moving forward, selecting tools and vendors for specific use cases, or building internal leadership consensus around a unified roadmap. Each framing requires a different type of engagement, and recognizing this upfront enables leaders to evaluate fit rather than credentials. The author emphasizes that outputs should be decision-grade rather than merely informative and encourages leaders to request sample deliverables from comparable engagements. The guide explicitly advises against providers who lead with model names, benchmark scores, or partner accreditations rather than evidence of business value.

## 500-word summary

Multimodal prompting represents an advanced approach to artificial intelligence interaction that extends significantly beyond traditional text-only communications by incorporating multiple types of data into a single coherent prompt structure. While conventional prompting relies solely on written instructions, multimodal prompting integrates text, images, code, audio, and video to enable more comprehensive and nuanced AI responses. This methodology recognizes that complex real-world scenarios, particularly in specialized domains like healthcare, rarely present information in a single format and therefore require a more sophisticated approach to capturing and processing information.

The health and wellness industry represents an ideal application area for multimodal prompting because wellness assessments inherently involve diverse data types that cannot be adequately captured through text alone. A typical health evaluation requires visual analysis of movement patterns to assess biomechanics and physical function, verbal communication between patients and providers to document symptoms and concerns, numerical data from laboratory tests and biometric measurements, and graphic visualizations that track progress over time. No single data modality can capture the full complexity of human health, making multimodal approaches essential for comprehensive assessment.

The article establishes three core pillars supporting multimodal health applications. The first pillar focuses on movement and performance analysis, combining visual data captured through video or imaging with textual instructions and code-based computational analysis to assess physical function and identify areas for improvement. The second pillar addresses smart nutrition systems by integrating food imagery—photographs of meals—with nutritional databases containing caloric and nutrient information, alongside personalized health data such as dietary restrictions and metabolic profiles to provide tailored nutritional guidance. The third pillar encompasses lifestyle medicine applications that merge multiple data streams, including sleep tracking data, physical activity metrics, stress biomarkers, and other physiological measurements to provide holistic health insights that consider the interconnected nature of various lifestyle factors.

For practitioners seeking to implement these concepts, the article provides a structured multimodal prompt template organized into six distinct components. The context section describes the overall goal and relevant background information that sets the stage for the interaction. The image input component specifies how visual data should be processed and interpreted, providing guidance on analyzing photographs, diagrams, or video frames. The text input section provides textual instructions, questions, or supplementary information that complements the visual data. Code integration explains how computational analysis should be applied to process and analyze the provided data, enabling quantitative assessments. Expected output defines the form the response should take, whether textual explanation, numerical results, or visual representations. Constraints specify any limitations or considerations that should guide the AI's response, ensuring appropriate boundaries are maintained. This systematic framework enables developers and health professionals to design effective multimodal AI systems that leverage diverse data sources for improved health outcomes.

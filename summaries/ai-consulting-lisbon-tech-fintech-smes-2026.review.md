The regulatory stack for AI systems in Portugal consists of four main layers. AI systems processing personal data require GDPR-compliant data processing agreements, legitimate legal bases, and typically a Data Protection Impact Assessment. Second, Banco de Portugal operates the FinLab Portugal innovation hub, a sandbox where fintech companies can engage informally with the regulator before launching regulated features like credit scoring models or AI-powered risk engines. Third, the EU AI Act applies directly in Portugal as an EU member state, with credit scoring systems classified as high-risk under Annex III, requiring conformity assessments, technical documentation, human oversight mechanisms, and EU database registration before deployment. Transparency obligations also require Portuguese language disclosures for consumer-facing AI applications. Fourth, CMVM adds additional requirements for companies in securities or investment advisory.

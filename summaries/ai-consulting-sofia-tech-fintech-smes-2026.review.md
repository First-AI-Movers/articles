The CPDP has intensified enforcement since 2024, focusing particularly on AI tools processing employee data and AI-assisted customer profiling in financial services. Bulgaria being an EU member state means the EU AI Act applies in full. The most immediately relevant obligations are the August 2026 deadline for general-purpose AI system documentation and Annex III deployer requirements for AI tools used in employment decisions or financial risk assessment. Sofia fintech companies deploying AI for credit scoring, fraud detection, or algorithmic trading support fall under Annex III Category 5b (financial services AI) or Category 6 (creditworthiness assessment), requiring full deployer documentation including human oversight designation, input data quality monitoring, incident logging, and basic conformity assessment. The third layer is BNB (Bulgarian National Bank) guidance. Fintech companies with BNB payment institution or e-money institution licenses must comply with BNB operational resilience requirements under PSD2 and, from 2025, DORA if they meet the DORA scope threshold. Sofia's tech market divides into three distinct profiles: software development and IT services companies (the largest segment, using developer productivity tools with low compliance risk), SaaS product companies (requiring provider-level compliance when their products include AI features), and fintech and payment companies (the highest-compliance segment for fraud detection, credit risk scoring, and KYC). Typical investment ranges from EUR 8,000-18,000 for full engagement or EUR 3,000-6,000 for compliance baseline and use case prioritization only. EU funding support includes OPSES (EU-funded digital transformation grants), InvestBulgaria Agency (advisory and investment incentive packages), and ICON 2021-2027 (co-financing for technology and innovation).

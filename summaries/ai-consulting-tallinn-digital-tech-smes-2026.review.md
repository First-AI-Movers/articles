SaaS product companies targeting European markets are export-oriented and adding AI features to product roadmaps, requiring provider compliance for AI capabilities shipped to customers. Cybersecurity and digital trust companies leverage AI for anomaly detection and threat hunting, with implications for how AI outputs are validated and explained to users. Fintech companies using AI for fraud detection and credit scoring face Annex III high-risk classification under the EU AI Act, triggering requirements for conformity assessments, quality management systems, and technical documentation.

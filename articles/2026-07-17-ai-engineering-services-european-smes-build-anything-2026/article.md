## How We Work

### Step 1: You Describe It

Tell us what you want to build. A product idea, a workflow problem, a competitive advantage you need. You do not need technical specifications, you need a clear description of the business outcome.

### Step 2: We Design the Architecture

Within one week, you receive:
- A system architecture document with component diagrams
- A technology selection rationale (why this model, this database, this hosting)
- A compliance assessment (GDPR, EU AI Act applicability)
- A cost estimate (development + monthly operating)
- A timeline with milestones

### Step 3: We Build in Sprints

2-week sprints with working deliverables at each milestone. You see progress, provide feedback, and steer direction. No disappearing for 6 months.

### Step 4: We Deploy and Operate

Your choice of deployment:
- **Sovereign**, on your own infrastructure, you own everything
- **Managed**, we deploy and operate it for you on EU-hosted cloud
- **Hybrid**, you own the infrastructure, we manage the application

### Step 5: We Stay or We Leave

After deployment:
- **Handoff**, full documentation, knowledge transfer, your team takes over
- **Retainer**, ongoing maintenance, updates, monitoring (Fractional CAIO model)
- **Growth**, continued feature development as your needs evolve

## Generative Engine Optimization: Built Into Everything We Publish

Every piece of content we create, for ourselves and for clients, is optimised for three search paradigms simultaneously:

| Paradigm | What It Means | How We Optimise |
|---|---|---|
| **SEO** | Traditional search engine ranking | Meta titles, descriptions, H2 structure, internal linking, schema markup |
| **AEO** | Answer Engine Optimization | FAQ sections, direct answer formatting, structured data, featured snippet targeting |
| **GEO** | Generative Engine Optimization | Front-loaded answers in the opening section, citation-friendly formatting, E-E-A-T signals, source-grounded claims |

Google AI Overviews now appear across a large and growing share of searches, and content that is well structured for machines is markedly more likely to be cited in AI-generated answers. Independent research from Princeton and collaborators, [GEO: Generative Engine Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735), found that optimising content for generative engines can lift its visibility in AI answers by up to 40% (S1).

We do not just write about GEO, we practice it. Our content engine has SEO and AEO validation built into the publish pipeline. Every article passes through quality gates that verify meta titles, FAQ sections, internal linking, and schema markup before it goes live.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you build an AI product if I only have a business idea, not technical specs?

Yes. That is the most common starting point. You describe the business outcome you want. We translate it into architecture, select the right technologies, and build it. The Discovery + Architecture phase exists specifically for this.

### How long does it take to get a working product?

MVP in 4-8 weeks. Full production platform in 3-6 months. We work in 2-week sprints with working deliverables at each milestone, you see progress from week 2, not month 6.

### Do I own the code?

Yes. You own everything we build. Full source code, documentation, and deployment scripts are handed over. No proprietary frameworks, no lock-in, no _you need us to run it_ traps.

### What about the EU AI Act?

Every system we build includes an EU AI Act risk classification. If your AI use case falls under Annex III (high-risk), we design the required human oversight, logging, and documentation from day one, not as a retrofit.

### Can you work with my existing engineering team?

Yes. We integrate as a fractional AI engineering team. Your developers participate in sprint reviews, learn the architecture, and can take over maintenance after handoff. We are not trying to replace your team, we are trying to accelerate it.

### What if I already have a platform and need AI added to it?

That is a common engagement. We assess your existing architecture, identify where AI creates the most value, and integrate it without rebuilding what works. Typical: add an AI-powered search, automate a manual workflow, or add content generation capabilities.
